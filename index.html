■ The roof

A glass box roof that works

The third floor sits under a glass box roof covered in solar panels. The sun powers the building, and the rain is caught and used to water the farm.

Solar roofPanels on the glass box turn sunlight into power for the lights and machines.
Glass boxLets daylight pour into the farm so the plants can grow.
Rain catchGutters collect rain from the roof into a big tank.
Water reuseThe stored rain waters the dye farm. Nothing is wasted.

Why Operaverse

Keeping Chinese Street Opera alive

How can a youth performance arcade in Singapore help bring Chinese Street Opera back to life by getting Gen Alpha and local artisans making, performing and selling together?

Opera is not something to keep behind glass. Here, a generation that grew up on games gets the tools to play it, build it and carry it forward.
